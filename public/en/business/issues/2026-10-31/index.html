Yaskawa and GAZOO Racing bring adaptive welding into production

Following a programmed welding path is not enough when workpieces vary. On October 28, Yaskawa Electric and Toyota's GAZOO Racing reported that an AI-assisted MOTOMAN NEXT system had entered production in September at a GR KART frame workshop in Gamagori, Japan. The announcement describes a working manufacturing process, not merely a laboratory demonstration.

AGIBOT wants to measure useful robot work, not just fleet size

A robot buyer needs useful output, not an impressive shipment count. At its October 29 event in Shanghai, AGIBOT introduced Worken, a proposed concept combining the words 'work' and 'token'. It aims to measure productive autonomous robot activity. The proposal has not become an independently established industry standard.

About the newspaper

The Replicant Truth is a digest from the world of physical artificial intelligence, created almost entirely with the help of AI.

This project is a tribute to Roy Batty from Blade Runner. To its author, Batty embodies a question that grows more urgent in our digital present: what makes us human — and how do we hold on to our humanity amid an avalanche of technological change?

Roy Batty and his famous monologue mean a great deal to me, as they do to many people. I claim no rights to the character, his screen portrayal or the monologue. The portrait and motto here are my own imaginative interpretation inspired by a much-loved film, offered as a tribute to the original. This is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with the creators or rights holders of Blade Runner.
