Issue glossary

Terms and definitions from the editorial dataset.

VLA

Vision-Language-Action model: turns visual observations and instructions into robot actions.

Tactile sensing

Measuring physical contact or force through sensors on a tool or hand.

Imitation learning

Learning a robot policy from demonstrated actions.

End-effector

A robot’s tool or gripper attached at the end of its arm.

Sim-to-real

Transfer of policies or insights from simulation to physical hardware.

Task graph

Representation of actions and the conditions on which later steps depend.

WES

Warehouse Execution System: coordinates equipment and operational work.

3D vision

Visual sensing that estimates object geometry and spatial placement.

Palletizing

Arranging boxes or goods into stable loads for transport.

Few-shot

Adapting to a new task from a small number of examples.

About the newspaper

The Replicant Truth is a digest from the world of physical artificial intelligence, created almost entirely with the help of AI.

This project is a tribute to Roy Batty from Blade Runner. To its author, Batty embodies a question that grows more urgent in our digital present: what makes us human — and how do we hold on to our humanity amid an avalanche of technological change?

Roy Batty and his famous monologue mean a great deal to me, as they do to many people. I claim no rights to the character, his screen portrayal or the monologue. The portrait and motto here are my own imaginative interpretation inspired by a much-loved film, offered as a tribute to the original. This is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with the creators or rights holders of Blade Runner.
