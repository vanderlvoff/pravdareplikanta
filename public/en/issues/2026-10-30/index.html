VLA-Stem adds reflex-like safety control without retraining the base VLA

A VLA can understand a command and still drive an arm into an obstacle. VLA-Stem addresses that gap without retraining the base model. It turns predicted action chunks into motion references and adds a faster local controller that adjusts execution using robot-state and obstacle observations.

TOPS accelerates flow-based manipulation without changing the network

Making manipulation faster does not always require a bigger neural network. TOPS changes where flow-based action generation begins and how it progresses, tailoring the source distribution and transport schedule to the task. The network architecture remains unchanged.

Read more… →

OpenJaws: open handheld demonstration gripper with stereo localization

Before a robot can learn a manipulation skill, someone must demonstrate it. OpenJaws records those examples without a robot: an operator moves a camera-equipped handheld gripper, and stereo vision reconstructs motion at metric scale. That avoids the absolute-scale ambiguity of monocular tracking.

Read more… →

RoboAuto searches robot programs by testing physical hypotheses

A robot may execute its program while failing the task: grasping empty space, for example, or proceeding before a drawer opens. RoboAuto puts physical verification into program search. It stores knowledge of objects and possible interactions, grounds it in the current scene and runs small experiments when competing explanations remain.

Read more… →

REACH teaches Unitree G1 to switch between walking and crawling

Walking is of little use when a passage is lower than a humanoid's torso. REACH gives the Unitree G1 a way to switch from bipedal movement to crawling and back. A transition policy bridges different contact modes, while LiDAR-based planning anticipates overhead clearance.

Read more… →

HC2Robot predicts footholds for humanoid parkour

Seeing the next foothold is not enough; a humanoid must anticipate when its foot will land and whether the surface can support it. HC2Robot predicts contact timing, position and quality from onboard observations, approximating information available only during simulator training. A Hyper-Connection Transformer combines the temporal signals.

Read more… →

LEAP hand reaches 95.2% of in-hand pose targets with fewer drops

A dexterous hand can reach the right pose and still drop its object. Researchers using the LEAP hand treat that risk as a separate safety problem. They train in simulation without human demonstrations and add an adjustable residual filter to moderate risky actions.

Read more… →

Japan: Tokio Marine launches coverage for AI-robot downtime

Robot deployment begins with productivity forecasts. It often ends with a harder question: who pays when the machine stops? Japan's Tokio Marine & Nichido has announced AI Robot Insurance for November 2026, covering specified downtime-related continuity costs alongside equipment damage and third-party liability.

Issue glossary

Terms and definitions from the editorial dataset.

VLA

Vision-Language-Action model: maps visual observations and language instructions to robot actions.

Action chunk

A short sequence of future motor commands predicted in one policy invocation.

Flow matching

A generative learning method that transforms a source distribution into action trajectories.

UMI

Universal Manipulation Interface: a handheld interface for collecting demonstrations without a robot.

SLAM

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping: estimating pose while building an environment map.

Affordance

An object property describing which interactions are physically possible.

Proprioception

Internal robot state sensing, including joint positions and velocities.

Sim-to-real

Transferring a controller learned in simulation to physical hardware.

TCO

Total Cost of Ownership: acquisition, operations, maintenance and downtime costs.
