REACH teaches Unitree G1 to switch between walking and crawling

Walking is of little use when a passage is lower than a humanoid's torso. REACH gives the Unitree G1 a way to switch from bipedal movement to crawling and back. A transition policy bridges different contact modes, while LiDAR-based planning anticipates overhead clearance.

Reported body-height commands span 1.27–0.50 m for walking and 0.50–0.20 m for crawling. The team demonstrates traversal on a physical G1. Inspection is an obvious possible application, but controlled demonstrations do not establish contact safety or long-term field reliability.

About the newspaper

The Replicant Truth is a digest from the world of physical artificial intelligence, created almost entirely with the help of AI.

This project is a tribute to Roy Batty from Blade Runner. To its author, Batty embodies a question that grows more urgent in our digital present: what makes us human — and how do we hold on to our humanity amid an avalanche of technological change?

Roy Batty and his famous monologue mean a great deal to me, as they do to many people. I claim no rights to the character, his screen portrayal or the monologue. The portrait and motto here are my own imaginative interpretation inspired by a much-loved film, offered as a tribute to the original.
