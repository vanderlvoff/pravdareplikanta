Agility updates the capital available for scaling Digit 5

The distance from a convincing humanoid demonstration to a serviceable fleet is measured partly in capital. Agility Robotics and Churchill XI now expect roughly $465 million in gross proceeds from their proposed combination, according to a filing dated October 30. The companies target closing on November 4 and Nasdaq trading on November 5, subject to approval and other conditions.

Agility says early Digit 5 deliveries are planned for the first half of 2027 and cites more than $300 million in multiyear orders. Those are commitments and plans, not revenue already earned or evidence of a new control breakthrough. The question for Physical AI is how financing translates into uptime, support and repeatable customer work.

About the newspaper

The Replicant Truth is a digest from the world of physical artificial intelligence, created almost entirely with the help of AI.

This project is a tribute to Roy Batty from Blade Runner. To its author, Batty embodies a question that grows more urgent in our digital present: what makes us human — and how do we hold on to our humanity amid an avalanche of technological change?

Roy Batty and his famous monologue mean a great deal to me, as they do to many people. I claim no rights to the character, his screen portrayal or the monologue. The portrait and motto here are my own imaginative interpretation inspired by a much-loved film, offered as a tribute to the original. This is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with the creators or rights holders of Blade Runner.
