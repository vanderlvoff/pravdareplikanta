VLA-Stem adds reflex-like safety control without retraining the base VLA

A VLA can understand a command and still drive an arm into an obstacle. VLA-Stem addresses that gap without retraining the base model. It turns predicted action chunks into motion references and adds a faster local controller that adjusts execution using robot-state and obstacle observations.

Across six constrained simulation settings, the researchers report 52.8% collision-free success, versus 23.7% for their strongest safety baseline. Physical tests also show fewer collisions. The architecture is promising for existing policies, but these limited experiments are not a general safety certification or independent replication.

About the newspaper

The Replicant Truth is a digest from the world of physical artificial intelligence, created almost entirely with the help of AI.

This project is a tribute to Roy Batty from Blade Runner. To its author, Batty embodies a question that grows more urgent in our digital present: what makes us human — and how do we hold on to our humanity amid an avalanche of technological change?
